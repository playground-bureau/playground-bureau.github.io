Dear Participant,

The Bureau's Recordkeeping Unit informs us that the Goodtimesiumometer has exploded due to record levels. It just couldn't handle it. Thank you very much for that—let's hope we were insured against joy.

But everybody at the Bureau agrees that September 24–27 in Solana de Riambau was a truly special time, and we are grateful to have co-created it with you.

After the debacle involving our former strategic research partner (regarding which the Syndicate were, for once, right), the Bureau has passed a resolution to never again take corporate sponsorships of any kind*.

*Exceptions may apply to Vichy Catalan. Vichy, are you reading this?

Where: Freedonia—Carrer de la Lleialtat 6, El Raval

RSVP TO ATTEND

The Syndicate told us that they are aiming for something uncharacteristically cozy at the front, with some party vibes at the back.

2. Presale for 2027

Monkey Business 4 will take place on September 23–26, 2027, at the same site, in the same strange world, but that's about all we know. If that's somehow enough info for you, there are 33 tickets at a reduced rate on our website. The rest of you can wait for registration to open in a few months.

3. T-Shirts

The Playground Bureau T-Shirt will be available for purchase at Freedonia for 25 euros with cash. This will help us build funds for future projects. Thank you for supporting the Playground Bureau!

4. Footage

The news crew who documented the weekend is compiling everything as we speak. In a month or so, we will release both still and moving pictures of the third annual programme. Meanwhile, please enjoy the VHS tapes recovered from the Syndicate, who simply abandoned them on site. They have been added to the Bureau archives.
