Registration Confirmed

MONKEY BUSINESS 4

Dear Participant,

Thanks for registering before anyone—us included—knows anything at all about what will happen next year. Of course, if for any reason the program does not take place, you'll get your money back, unless we manage to move all funds to the Cayman Islands before you get to us. We are so grateful for the amount of trust you've placed in the Bureau. Perhaps you caught Stockholm Syndrome at Monkey Business 3. Either way, it means a lot. Thank you.

1. What We Know For Sure
  • Program: Playground Bureau's Monkey Business 4
  • Dates: September 23rd–26th, 2027
  • Gates open: Thursday, September 23rd at 14:00
  • Gates close: Sunday, September 26th at 18:00
  • Location: Solana de Riambau, about two hours from Barcelona: https://maps.app.goo.gl/uVfU7gHi14eYs3Tn7
2. What Will be the Same
  • You camp. Tents on site, showers and bathrooms provided (we have plans to improve them). Vans and RVs are welcome but capacity is limited, so let us know by email beforehand. There may be some lodging options made available. 
  • The bar runs on you. We buy the kegs, mixers and ice; you bring the bottles. Donate according to how much you plan to drink, and we serve it all back free of charge. This time we might do a bit of coordination so we end up with the right stock of booze. 
  • Bring your own plate, cup and cutlery. The Bureau won't have them for everyone, if you are a fan of eating, then we highly recommend you bring your own stuff.
  • Phones away. No photos on the dancefloor, no computers on the grounds. Most carriers have no reception anyway. We highly discourage onsite doom-scrolling, it's so not cool. 
  • Zero tolerance. No discrimination of any kind and no harassment, sexual or otherwise. We reserve the right to remove anyone from our premises as a private cultural association. We do not tolerate sexism, racism, homophobia, Nazism, Zionism, and all other forms of discriminatory or imperialistic rhetoric that goes against our values of bringing people together.  
3. What's Coming Next

Closer to the date, you'll receive the full participant guide: shuttle tickets, lodgings, packing list, gate hours and everything else we currently know nothing about. Until then, there is nothing you need to do, except tell us ASAP if you have any serious allergies.

If you have any questions, feel free to reach out via email or Instagram.

Much love,
