MB-3 Workshops & Activities Program

Breathwork Journey: Freeing the Monkey
Facilitator: Bénédicte

In this breathwork, we get intimate with our inner world, through breath and presence, we'll explore where seriousness, self-consciousness, or old 'shoulds' have crowded out play. We will invite space to loosen the grip so the silly, spontaneous, monkey part of you has room to come out and be!

Capture the Flag
Facilitators: Luca & Sophia

A big ole' game of Capture the Flag.

Chair 101
Facilitator: Tommycraft

Build an Enzo Mari style chair from glue and screws and learn how to sand and varnish wood.

Comedy Workshop
Facilitator: Amy Walsh

If you've ever wanted to try stand up comedy—now's your chance. We're going to do a wee comedy writing workshop and then a little performance of the workshop participants. Low risk, low pressure! Let's see if you're funny on the stage as well as off!

Cosmic Collage
Facilitator: Sonia Blough

Step into a space of play, presence and creative freedom. Cosmic collage is an invitation to express yourself through intuitive art-making: no experience or "talent" required, just a desire to reflect, connect, and imagine. All materials provided.

Danza Sutil / Subtle Dance
Facilitator: Joana Torello

Subtle Dance is a practice that integrates somatics, crystal therapy, and dance to explore the body through listening, presence, and expression. Crystals are used as a supportive tool to enhance the process, facilitate inner regulation, and accompany what the body needs to release. Movement and dance emerge as pathways to create inner space, release tension, and give form to what moves within us.

Earth, Hands & Fire ~ A Primitive Pottery Workshop
Facilitator: Alexandra Pfeiffer

Join us in the OG pottery studio: the land <3 Delight your senses as you create with clay, nature prints, foraged tools, and explore what humanity is all about. Play, touch, feel, love.

Herbal Mixology
Facilitator: Alexa

Part tasting, part experiment. Explore the herbs behind the bar, draw a mystery prompt, and mix your own functional drink. The formula is yours to discover.

Kung Flow: Building Support / Releasing Power
Facilitator: Gonzalo

This workshop invites us to experience ways of organising our intention and body movement to build supporting and dynamic structures on our body, and at the same time to organise relaxation and openness to allow movement into the space. Using Kung Fu concepts combined with contemporary dance improvisation and contact impro tasks, we will dive into a powerful and subtle game of dancing, caring and daring.

Living in Alignment: Find Out What Really Matters
Facilitator: Lea Banana

Ever felt like something's "off" despite everything looking good on paper? Explore your core values, reflect on alignment, and gain clarity, inspiration, and ideas for meaningful change.

Mushroom Identification and Foraging
Facilitator: Elia

A light-hearted ~30 minutes introductory presentation to the art of mushroom identification and then a deep dive in the forest to put in practice the theory. You can come to both parts or just one. If you come to forage in the forest, wear appropriate shoes and clothes. Shoes and clothes are optional during the presentation. Maybe we won't find any mushrooms, but mushroom-friends are for life!

Sacred Self-Discovery with Life Drawing
Facilitator: Fede Art Muse

The workshop is an invitation to surrender, explore and get surprised through life drawing. I invite people to discover themselves by connecting to their creative flow and their inner child immersed in an electronic and tribal music while drawing a flow of nude time limited poses.

Twerkshop—A Sacred Shake
Facilitator: Ronke

Open, soften and awaken your hips through yoga, breath and embodied movement before diving into the joyful, liberating practice of twerking, rhythm and self-expression.
