1. Overview ★
Greetings, friends back in 2026. This is the Bureau, transmitting from the year 2027. We hope this message makes it through.
Our readings indicate that conditions in 2026 have become increasingly cold and dark.
But fear not! Things are looking much brighter over here in 2027, and the Bureau has arranged approximately one (1) journey to bring you over.
It departs late December 2026, and all participants will be transported into 2027 almost instantaneously, as if by ✨MAGIC✨ (or the power of friendship, maybe? We still haven't quite figured out how this works).
There will be no second (2nd) departure. Miss this one, and you may be stuck in the past forever.
For the journey, the Bureau is thrilled to provide:
  • Three (3) rooms, each with its own flavour of music to adorn the airwaves
  • Beautiful decor to feed your eyes
  • A community bar to keep you lubricated
  • Approximately 80 fellow time travellers
  • And many surprises along the way
2. Community Bar 🍸
Here's how it works:
  • Bring roughly what you think you'll drink for the night (a bottle of spirits, wine, beers, whatever you fancy) and hand it in at the bar when you arrive.
  • The Bureau provides the base: beers and soft drinks.
  • Everyone's donations do the rest, and together we end up with a fully stocked bar.
  • Everything is served to everyone, free of charge.
Please Note
Dinner is not included. Eat before the journey.
