2. Important Announcements 

Food & Allergies

IMPORTANT 1 : If you have any serious allergies, please inform us ASAP (if you haven't already done so when you purchased your ticket). You can reach us via email or instagram.

IMPORTANT 2: Do not forget to bring your own plate + cutlery + a cup. The Bureau won't have them for everyone. 

Community Open Bar

Our Bar runs on drinks gifted by you. We buy the kegs, mixers, ice, and you bring the liquor, wine, liqueueuer, Vichy Catalan, etc. We serve them back to you free of charge. Each participant is expected to donate bottles to the bar according to the amount they plan to drink. That means big drinkers should donate multiple big bottles to the bar, and tiny, miniature drinkers should donate tiny amounts of tiny bottles.

This system can only work when everybody does their part. Please do not show up empty handed!

Gate Hours

The Bureau highly recommends grabbing Thursday afternoon off.  Arrive before sunset for easier tent-pitching and an early start. Thursday will begin with our usual Opening Act—you don't want to miss it!

We really want to avoid congestion near the entrance portal during peak arrival times on Thursday. 19:00–20:00 will likely be rush hour. Leave work early if you can, or have your parents call in sick to your office.

Opening Hours Recap : 
Thursday  Hours : 14h00-00h00 
Friday Hours : 11h00-22h00
Saturday Hours : Closed 
Sunday Hours : Closed 

If you arrive outside of these, please let us know.

The festival will close on Sunday at 6pm and we will begin teardown. While we appreciate that some might want to stay longer, we will close the festival at said time. If you wish to stay longer to help with cleaning up let us know in advance, there might be some opportunities to sign up for some odd jobs. 

Cigarette Butts

With the very dry summer, we kindly ask everyone to be extra careful with cigarette butts and always use the ashtrays that will be made available throughout the festival grounds. This will greatly minimise the risk of fire hazard as well as being respectful of the beautiful location we enjoy.  

3. Getting There

Shuttle (Bureau Transit Division)

There is a shuttle from Barcelona to the festival (and back). Seats are limited, so grab yours now before they vanish. Please, read mindfully the tickets description before purchasing.

  • Departure: 24th of September - at 17h In front of Estació de França
  • Return: 27th of September around 17h

Grab your bus ticket here: HERE

Driving

If you are driving over, no problem at all, you will be able to park your car. But we don't want every participant driving up solo when you can carpool! You can join the carpool chat on the Whatsapp Community Group to find a driver or a rider to get to the venue. 

The drive from Barcelona city center is about 2 hours.

Train

Alternatively, you can board the train from Plaça Espanya and ride it to Girona or Figueres station (1h30–2h00). From there, you can catch a taxi to the venue. The taxi fare should be approximately 50–60 euros.

4. Mandatory Disclosure

The Bureau will now break character just for one paragraph. Enjoy it while it lasts. Monkey Business has an ongoing interactive story, and you'll be able to mingle with characters played by members of our actors team. If they talk to you, play along! There's no wrong way. You can also simply watch or not participate at all—we will never oblige you to do anything. But please do not intentionally try to break their character, out of respect for their craft and the people playing along around you. If you want to come prepared, you can read up on our history. 

Aaaand that concludes mandatory disclosure as mandated by Bureau Directive 455d! While we're at it, Sponsorship Agreement Section 9.2d requires us to state just how "filled to the brim with positive emotions" we are to be working with Carrefoogle Corporation this year, our official event sponsor. They also asked us to inform you that they are showcasing their yet-to-be-released embodied companion product, Bestee 3.0, at Monkey Business.

Everything's fine. Moving along!

5. The Funemployment Office 

Do you want to take on fun shifts during the festival? You'll be able to find out what's available and put your name down at the Funemployment Office. Shifts include bar, canteen, Post Office, and more. We have a 100% acceptance rate. 

6. Saturday Theme: Denim Day 

As per last year, the Bureau has decided on a little theme for the Saturday at the festival. This year will be Denim. So bring on your retro, your cowboy, your colorful, your double denim best.

7. Accommodation

Camping

Designated camping areas are available onsite with plenty of space for your tent. Camping areas are situated at a sufficient distance from music stages to permit rest. If you don't have a tent, feel free to ask in the WhatsApp group - someone might have a spare.

IMPORTANT: RVs/vans are also welcome, but please let us know via email beforehand since capacity is limited.

Lodgings

8. On Site

Showers & Bathrooms

The site is equipped with showers and bathrooms, complete with mirrors to help you get festival-ready. Don't forget to bring your own towel and toiletries!

Phone & Computer Guidelines

Disconnect from your phone, reconnect with actual humans. The scenery is photo-worthy, but no unsolicited pics of people mid-boogie. Think Berlin nightclub rules: no photos on the dancefloor. 

A photographer will be on-site to capture moments worth framing.

Most carriers have no reception here, by the way. Let your family and friends know you'll be off the grid so they don't worry.

We also have a no computer policy on the ground. 

Substance Use

We hope this goes without saying, but we prioritize safety and respect for all participants. While we acknowledge some may choose to partake in substances, we urge you to do so responsibly. Use common sense and help keep the vibes positive and safe.

9. Bureau Policy

We state it clearly on our website, but this is a friendly reminder: we do not tolerate any discrimination based on gender, sexuality, or identity, nor any form of harassment—sexual or otherwise. We reserve the right to remove anyone from our premises.

Dedicated staff will be available to address any concerns that may arise, alongside security to handle inappropriate or harmful behaviour. If you witness or experience something that doesn't feel right, please let us know. We will always respect your privacy and your choices.

10. Can't Make It Anymore?

If you want to sell your ticket, please contact us via email. We will offer it to the people on the waitlist. We will reimburse your ticket fully and directly into your account once someone from the waiting list has confirmed they'll buy it. 

11. Packing Guide

Cups, Plates & Utensils

The Bureau kindly asks you to BYOPCU (Bring Your Own Plate, Cup, and Utensils). If you are flying in from abroad or have other reasons why this is difficult for you, please let us know so we can plan accordingly.

WHAT TO PACK:

  • Your ID
  • A cup
  • Plate + cutlery / something to eat out of/with
  • BEVERAGES TO DONATE to the community bar (more info above)
  • Warm clothes! It's 12–14 degrees at night.
  • Toiletries - there are showers on site! Eco-friendly if possible
  • Caring, open, and silly mindset.
  • Swimwear
  • Denim for Saturday if possible 
  • Umbrella if it rains 

CAMPING GEAR:

  • Tent, sleeping bag / cosy bedding
  • Torch
  • Rain protection, rain coat (just in case!)
  • Your favourite water bottle
  • Mosquito repellent
  • Mosquito repellent (saying it twice because it's important)
  • TOWEL / TOOTHBRUSH

NICE-TO-HAVES:

  • Floaties 
  • Vitamins (C, B-12, Magnesium)—it will help with your hangovers!
  • Festival outfits

THINGS NOT TO PACK:

  • Fireworks
  • Surströmming
